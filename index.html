The island

Where mountains meet the sea

Taiwan is an island of extraordinary contrasts. Alpine peaks rise above subtropical forests while rivers cut through deep valleys and the Pacific Ocean stretches endlessly beyond the coastline. For nature lovers, the island is a world waiting to be explored.

Eastern Taiwan

Taroko

Marble cliffs, rushing rivers and dramatic mountain valleys create one of Taiwan's most iconic landscapes.

Pacific Coast

The Blue Horizon

Follow the coastline and discover quiet beaches, fishing villages and the endless Pacific horizon.

Mountain Forests

Into the Green

Ancient forests, misty trails and mountain ecosystems make Taiwan a paradise for hikers and wildlife lovers.
